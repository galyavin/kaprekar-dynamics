# Frozen finite probes for SETTLE-35

A draw contributes to the denominator even if it is censored or leaves a guarded domain. The population theorem requires a separate all-scale certificate and original-word count. The [original freeze](FORECAST-AS-FROZEN.txt) retains the bytes recorded before scoring. This display edition restores the mathematical typesetting.

1. Independent roots at length 35. Draw 96 points uniformly with replacement from each integer cube \([-L,L]^{12}\) for \(L=64,128,256,512,1024,2048,4096\). SplitMix64 seeds are \(350000+L\), one stream per scale. Allow \(512L\) forward steps to detect a repeat by Brent's algorithm. Log entry and least period, maximum lifetime root spread including the anchor, loss through entry, and loss per recurrent period. The budgeted event is a detected zero-export repeat with lifetime spread at most \(32L\) and total export at most \(512L\). Apply the same budgeted event to the autonomous projections of physical draws, with their own denominator and the same step cap. A censored draw is counted as unknown for the falling-share criterion.
2. Paired source-box comparison at length 35. Use \(B=200000,2000003,20000000\), \(L=\lfloor B/10000\rfloor\), 24 fresh draws at each base, and seeds \(351000+B\). Each of the 17 non-anchor gaps has its own uniform integer offset in \([-L,L]\). Use centre root level \(\lfloor B/20\rfloor\), complementary excess \(\lfloor B/50\rfloor\), and make the anchor enforce the sum. Follow literal subtraction for up to \(512L\) steps for cycle detection. Compare the same source IDs with their autonomous roots through the uninterrupted enlarged chamber \(C^*\). Record least entry, period, exact peak numerator, whether all terminal phases lie in \(C\) or \(C^*\), first loss of full height, pairing or an enabled guard, first return to \(C^*\) after a leave, first \(d_2\) breach and return to \(C\) from it, and agreement and cumulative per-label export on the uninterrupted section. Track every row's minimum on each compared prefix. Cuts are strict \(Z_B>1/1000\) and \(Z_B>1/200\). Keep the external \(L=20000\) results separate.
3. Falling-share criterion. In the independent root cubes only, pool \(L\le256\) and \(L\ge1024\) separately. Declare a finite decline only if the high-scale share falls by at least 10 percentage points even when every censored high-scale draw counts as a success and each censored low-scale draw counts as a failure. The intermediate scale \(512\) is shown separately. This criterion makes no asymptotic claim.
4. Second lengths. Freeze their integer profile certificates, cut, visibility control and budgets before scoring either one. A pinned row may breach in the full-dimensional box, and is reported separately from loss of pairing and the other rows. All stated successes use literal subtraction.

The forward cap is a detection cap. Post-detection reconstruction of the least entry and one full cycle, and the paired comparison over those known phases, are separate passes. One scale is the normal compute tranche and the largest can be split. Use the project lock and copy each completed tranche's small output from its private directory to the evidence directory.
