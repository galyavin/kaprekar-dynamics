# Task 098 analytic proof checks

On the 18 positive-dimensional regular boundary words, check one
nonnegative row of the active comparison matrix per word. At the
strictly paired Two interior, check all four branch matrices of its
two-step return from the primitive eleven positions and compare to the
stated tangential and transverse formulas. Verify the squared and
fourth-powered contraction bounds for the induced normal return, and
the rational constants in the neighborhood and original-volume bound.
All checks use exact integer and rational arithmetic.
